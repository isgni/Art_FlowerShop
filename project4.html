Terms and Agreement

Art & Lovey’s Principles, Boundaries, and Shared Commitments
Between: Art Pimentel & Lovelyn Jaime Date: August 23, 2026

We, the two of us, with affection and respect for one another, have decided to make this Condition and terms or Agreement. We are making this not as a legal contract or a forced commitment, but as a sincere expression of our principles, boundaries, and commitment while we continue getting to know each other and prepare for a possible serious relationship in the future. We want to base this agreement on Bible principles and strive to make Jehovah God the center of our decisions and the way we treat each other.

💙 Art Pimentel's Terms & Agreement

1. Faithfulness and Loyalty
1 Cor. 13:4-7
We will not entertain romantic intentions from other people while our relationship continues. “Love continues to trust, hope, and endure.”
Commitment I will strive to remain faithful and avoid doing anything that could damage your trust in me.
2. Honesty
Eph. 4:25
We will not hide important matters that could affect our understanding of each other even if it's difficult. “Speak truth with one another.”
Commitment I will choose honest and sincere communication rather than pretending.
3. Clear Relationship Status
Matt. 5:37
We recognize that we have mutual feelings and intentions toward each other, but formal courtship will begin only when brother ready to do through action. “Let your word Yes mean yes, and your No mean no.”
Commitment I will remain clear and honest about my intentions toward you and will not give you false expectations or mix signals.
4. Personal Priorities
Phil. 2:4
We will respect each other's work, family, spiritual studies, spiritual responsibilities, and personal goals. “Look out not only for your own interests but also for the interests of others.”
Commitment I will support your responsibilities and will not try to control your time or priorities.
5. No Pressure
1 Cor. 13:4
We will not pressure each other to make decisions before we are fully ready. Love and waiting should come from free and sincere willingness. “Love is patient and enduring.”
Commitment I will not pressure you to do something you are not yet ready to do.
6. Respect for Boundaries
1 Pet. 2:17
We will respect each other's personal space, privacy, time, family, decisions, and personal boundaries. “Honor people of all sorts.”
Commitment I will respect you as a person who has your own decisions and boundaries.
7. Good Communication
James 1:19
We will strive to communicate and check on each other regularly, while also understanding when one of us is busy. When there is a problem, we will not make it worse through anger or unnecessary withdrawal.
Commitment I will listen to you before responding, and I will try to express my feelings in a calm and respectful way.
8. Resolving Misunderstandings
Col. 3:13
When we have a problem or disagreement, we will look for a calm and respectful way to resolve it. We will not insult, threaten, manipulate, or use withdrawal as punishment. “Continue putting up with one another and forgiving one another.”
Commitment When we have a problem, I will choose communication and resolve the problem rather than making the problem worse.
9. Do Not Judge Too Quickly
Prov. 18:13
If we see or hear something that causes suspicion, we will ask each other and listen to the explanation before making a conclusion. It is not good to answer a matter before hearing it and understanding it.
Commitment I will not immediately make conclusions based only on what I see or hear. I will listen to you first.
10. Preparing for the Future
Prov. 16:3
Our plan to be together in the future and possibly get married is a sincere intention, not a guarantee or obligation. Our goal is to serve Jehovah together while growing. For brother, he will strive to become responsible and prepared when the time comes for me to begin courting you.
Commitment I will strive to become responsible in my life, work, spirituality, and finances while praying for the right direction for our future.
11. Freedom to Express Feelings
Eph. 4:15
If either of us develops different feelings, that person has the freedom to honestly and respectfully express them. We will not pressure, threaten, or blame each other because of it. “Speak the truth with love.”
Commitment I will give you the freedom to be honest about your feelings, even if what you say is not what I want to hear.
12. Growing Together
1 Thess. 5:11
We will help each other become better people—in spirituality, character, responsibility, work, studies, and the way we treat others. “Keep encouraging and building one another up.”
Commitment I do not want our closeness to become a burden to your growth. I want to be a positive influence in your life.

Our Personal Commitment

We are not making this agreement as a forced obligation, but as an expression of our respect, trust, faithfulness, and sincere intentions. While we wait for the time when we are ready for a more serious relationship, we will strive to follow Bible principles, respect each other's boundaries, and give each other enough freedom and opportunity to fulfill our individual responsibilities and continue growing.

"We will trust in Jehovah with all our heart and seek his guidance in our path." — Proverbs 3:5, 6

🌷 Lovely Jaime's Terms & Agreement

I, Lovey, am making these terms and agreements because I value you, our connection, and the possibility of our future together. We have chosen to wait until we are both truly ready. I respect that decision, and I want our time of waiting to be meaningful—not a period where either of us is confused, deceived, or taken for granted. Mahalaga sa akin ang trust and assurance. Ayokong maulit yung sakit na pinagdaanan ko dahil lang sa misunderstandings, lies, or someone not being honest about their intentions.

1. Our Spiritual Goals
I want us to continue working toward our spiritual goals and becoming better people individually and together. I want us to encourage each other, support each other's growth, and never use our spiritual goals as a reason to manipulate, pressure, or control one another.
2. We Give Each Other Time
I understand na kailangan din natin ng time for ourselves. I will respect your personal space, and I expect the same from you.
3. Keep Our Commitment to Each Other
While we are waiting for the right time, I ask that you keep your heart and intentions clear toward me. As much as possible do not give another girl romantic attention or lead someone else to believe that you are interested in them.
4. Assurance
Hindi ko hinihingi na lagi mong patunayan yung feelings mo sa akin. I just want your words and actions to match.
5. Loyalty
For me, loyalty means being transparent, protecting our trust, and not secretly doing something na alam mong makakasakit sa akin o against sa commitment natin.
6. Respect
I want our connection to always have respect. Respect my feelings, boundaries, personal space, opinions, friendships, family, and decisions. I will respect yours too. Hindi tayo dapat nag-iinsult, nanghihiya, nagma-manipulate, nananakot, or intentionally nananakit sa isa't isa.
7. Clear & Honest Communication
  • If something bothers you, tell me.
  • If something changes, tell me.
  • If your feelings change, tell me.
  • If you're confused about us, tell me.

I would rather hear an uncomfortable truth from you than a beautiful lie.

8. Please Answer My Questions Honestly
Sometimes I overthink because of the things I've experienced before. Kapag may tinatanong ako because I'm genuinely worried or uncertain, please answer me honestly and patiently. Hindi ko hinihingi na ibigay mo lahat ng privacy mo or constantly prove yourself to me. I just want to know na kapag may genuine concern ako, I can trust your answer.
9. No False Promises
Please don't promise me a future just to make me stay. If you truly want a future with me, let your actions show it.
10. If We Make a Mistake
We're both human, and mistakes can happen. If either of us breaks an important part of this agreement, we should talk about it honestly instead of hiding it.
